# Requirements

## Functional requirements (what the system must DO)
1. The system shall allow students to input, update, and submit their housing preferences (including budget range, preferred room/rental type, max distance to UiTM Samarahan campuses, and required amenities).
2. The system shall allow administrators or owners to add, edit, and delete housing listings along with their corresponding feature vector attributes (e.g., rental price, location coordinates, amenities list, images, and owner contact details).
3. The system shall allow students to manually filter and search listings by specific keywords, exact rental price ranges, or room availability status.
4. The system shall display a personalized, ranked list of housing recommendations based on highest similarity scores, complete with match score percentages and property summaries.
5. The system shall provide detailed view pages for each recommended property, enabling students to inspect full feature breakdowns and directly initiate contact with property owners.

## Non-functional requirements (how the system must PERFORM)
1. The system shall maintain an operational availability of 99% during peak student intake and semester registration periods.
2. The system shall process student preference inputs and display ranked housing recommendations within 2 seconds.
3. The system shall feature a responsive user interface that scales seamlessly across mobile devices, tablets, and desktop browsers.
