# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Non-resident UiTM students who fail to secure on-campus college accommodation, as well as individuals looking to rent residential properties in the Samarahan area.

## What exactly is going wrong?
The rental search process relies heavily on unstructured channels like Facebook groups and direct messaging with property owners. Students must manually inquire about essential details—such as rental rates, location, rules, and amenities—for every individual listing. This reliance on fragmented communication results in repetitive inquiries, exposure to outdated or unavailable listings, and severe information overload.

## Why does it matter?
This manual search creates significant time loss, financial uncertainty, and severe stress during peak intake periods. Lacking a centralized, personalized matching tool that make students often experience decision paralysis and are forced to settle for unsuitable, overpriced, ultimately impacting their daily commute, living conditions, and academic performance.
